# SA Track — How to use it

A plain-language guide for everyone. (Also available inside the app: **profile menu → How to use**.)

## The 3 rules that matter most

1. **Clock in when you start, clock out when you leave.** This is your “in office” time.
2. **You must be clocked in to track.** The desktop tracker only runs while you’re clocked in and not on a break.
3. **Clock out when you’re done.** If you forget, the system closes your day at your shift end automatically — but clocking out yourself keeps your hours accurate.

---

## Clocking in & out (your attendance)

Your **clock** records when you’re present at work. Clock in/out from the **Dashboard** (web) or the **desktop app** — it’s the same clock.

- **Clock In** — when you start your work day.
- **Clock Out** — when you finish for the day.

> “In Office” on the dashboard = the time between your clock-in and clock-out (minus breaks). It is **not** the same as tracked work time.

## Tracking your work (desktop app)

The desktop app records your active work — screenshots, activity %, keystrokes/clicks — for the client and task you pick.

1. Open the desktop app and **Clock In** (the shift bar at the bottom).
2. Pick a client, add a short description, and press the big **Start** button.
3. Press **Stop** when you switch tasks or finish.

> If the Start button is greyed out, it’ll say **“Clock in to start tracking.”** Clock in first (or end your break), then start.

## Breaks

Going on a break? Press **Start Break** — this pauses tracking and your break time doesn’t count as work. Press **End Break**, then **Resume**, when you’re back.

## Adding time manually (Work Diary)

For work the tracker didn’t capture (a meeting, offline work, a call), add it by hand:

1. Go to **Work Diary → Add** (or the “Add Manual Time” button).
2. Pick the client/work type, write what you did, and enter the **hours and minutes**.
3. Save.

> Time the desktop app tracked is **locked** — you can’t hand-edit those hours. If a tracked entry is wrong, remove the relevant screenshots from the Timeline instead.

## Changing your shift for one day (My Schedule)

Need a different shift on a specific day — e.g. start earlier on Friday because you’re off Saturday? If you have access, open **My Schedule**:

1. Pick the date (today or a future day).
2. Set the new start time and/or length, and an optional reason.
3. Save. That one day uses your new shift; every other day stays normal.

> This only moves your schedule for that day. It never adds hours. Don’t have My Schedule? Ask an admin to enable it for you.

## What the system does automatically

- **Forgot to clock out?** The system closes your day at your **shift end + a short grace period**, so a forgotten clock-out doesn’t inflate your hours. If you’re genuinely still working (tracker running), it leaves you alone.
- **Still working late?** You may get a friendly Slack “still working?” check. Reply, or it closes your day after a few unanswered nudges.
- **Clocked out but tracker still on?** The tracker stops itself — you can’t record time outside a clock-in.

## Quick answers

- **“Why did my tracker stop on its own?”** — You (or the system) clocked you out, or your session was closed at shift end. Clock in again to keep tracking.
- **“Why can’t I press Start?”** — You’re not clocked in, or you’re on a break. Clock in / end your break first.
- **“My in-office time looks too high.”** — You probably forgot to clock out on a previous day. Clock out when you leave; the system also auto-closes forgotten days at shift end.
- **“Tracked time vs in-office?”** — In-office = clock-in to clock-out (presence). Tracked = what the desktop app actually recorded. They’re different on purpose.

## For admins & managers

- **Fix someone’s clock times** — if an employee forgot to clock in/out, open **Attendance → Summary → Edit clock times** (needs the permission). Every edit is recorded in the audit history.
- **Change anyone’s shift for a day** — with “Change anyone’s shift” you can set or backdate a one-day shift for any employee.
- **Grant access** — Super Admins enable features per person on the **Users** page (clock-time editing, My Schedule, reports, screenshots, etc.).
- **Reading the numbers** — the Dashboard team table shows In-Office vs Tracked vs Activity %. A big gap between in-office and tracked usually means present-but-not-tracking (meetings, offline work) or a forgotten clock-out.
